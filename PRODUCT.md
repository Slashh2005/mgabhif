## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js + Tailwind CSS (user's choice). Static export so it can be hosted anywhere.

## Users

Inferred from the copy (not separately confirmed): B2B buyers and partners evaluating a supplier —
procurement directors at coal / chrome offtake partners, mine operations leads hiring plant or
sub-contractors, fuel buyers for remote sites, and international commodity buyers. They visit to
judge whether Mgabhi is credible, safe and capable, then make contact.

## Product Purpose

Corporate marketing site for Mgabhi & Famaly Construction Pty Ltd (mgabhi.co.za). Success is a
qualified visitor starting a partnership conversation (phone, email or enquiry).

## Positioning

A South African, family-founded company working across four verticals at once — plant hire,
commodity export (coal, chrome, copper), petroleum / diesel supply and mining services — in five
named regions: Mpumalanga, Middelburg, Rustenburg, Mooinooi, Gauteng.

## Operating Context

Stockpiles, wash plants and spiral concentrators, coal rail sidings, diesel bulk tanks and tankers,
site engineers in hi-vis. Head office: 22 Magwa Crescent, Waterfall, Midrand, Gauteng.

## Brand Commitments

- Name, confirmed by the user: **Mgabhi & Famaly Construction Pty Ltd**. "Famaly" is the deliberate

- Founded: **Est. 2022** (from the logo; overrides the copy doc's 2015).
- Logo: mine-cart train leaving a tunnel arch, charcoal on white, navy wordmark
  (`public/images/logo-mgabhi-CUkKpKU7.jpg`). Colours follow the logo: charcoal, white, navy.
- Badges: B-BBEE Level 1, Safety First Policy.

  5 Operating Regions, 4 Commodity Verticals.
- Partners confirmed by the user: Exxaro, Black Wattle, Mooinooi, Mining Indaba, Mpumalanga DMR

## Product Principles

1. Credibility first: real sites, real people, real numbers — nothing invented.
2. Every vertical says plainly what is supplied and where.
3. Make contact effortless: phone, email and the office address on every screen that asks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
